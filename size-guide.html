Find the right fit across our full range, from toddler through adult sizing. All measurements are in inches.

How to Measure

For the most accurate fit, measure a similar, well-fitting garment and compare to the tables below. Chest/bust is measured at the fullest point, waist at the natural waistline, and hip at the fullest point of the hip. Inseam is measured from the crotch to the hem.
