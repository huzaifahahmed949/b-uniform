Shipping & Returns

Shipping: Orders are processed and shipped promptly once a quote is confirmed. Shipping timelines and costs vary depending on order size and destination — your quote will include an estimated delivery window.

Returns: If an item arrives damaged or doesn't match your order, contact us within 14 days of delivery at buniform@outlook.com and we'll arrange a replacement or resolution. Because orders are quote-based and often produced for reseller quantities, please confirm sizes and colours carefully before finalizing your order.

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information necessary to respond to your inquiries and fulfill orders — such as your name, business name, email, and phone number submitted through our contact and quote forms. This information is used solely to communicate with you about your request and is never sold to third parties.

If you have questions about how your information is used or would like it removed from our records, contact us at buniform@outlook.com.

Terms of Service

By requesting a quote or placing an order with B-Uniform, you agree to provide accurate information regarding sizes, quantities, and contact details. Pricing provided in quotes is valid for a limited time and is subject to confirmation at the time of order.

All product descriptions, images, and specifications on this site are provided for reference; minor variations in fabric or color may occur between production runs. For questions about a specific order, please reach out before confirming.
