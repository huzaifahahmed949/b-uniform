About B-Uniform

Quality garments for every season — built for resellers who need consistency, durability, and a full size range.

Our Story

Built on quality, made for resellers

B-Uniform supplies a diverse range of quality garments to resellers, ensuring every piece is presented and constructed to a modern standard. What started as a focused catalog of polos and outerwear has grown into a full uniform program spanning polos, hoodies, jackets, sweatshirts, sweatpants, pants, joggers, and shorts.

We know that resellers and schools need more than a single style in a single size — they need a catalog that covers every grade, every body, and every season. That's why our range runs from toddler 3T through adult XXL, with consistent quality across every size we offer.
